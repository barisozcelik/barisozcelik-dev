Trial and purchase

Try all features free for three days from first use. After the trial, the clock stays visible. Scan the code on the watch for a one-time US$2.99 purchase to unlock the full face. The trial ends without an automatic charge.

Privacy policy

Palm Drive reads activity, step goal, heart rate and battery information on the watch to draw your selected fields. These readings stay on your watch.

To manage the trial and paid access, the face sends WearCheckout the Garmin-provided app-specific watch identifier, system language, model part number, library version and watch face indicator. The watch stores trial and unlock information locally. WearCheckout uses transaction and refund status to update access. Polar processes the contact, billing and payment details entered at checkout.

For support, refunds and privacy requests, email Barış Özçelik with your watch code or order reference. You can request access, correction or deletion subject to applicable retention requirements.
